# Provenance and preservation

The three originals were supplied by Devin Endorphin in this project’s originating conversation. Their filenames and complete bytes are retained in `originals/`.

The boat transcript shows Gemini responding to a modified mathematics-teaching example: teach an uninformed learner who barely speaks human language and resists instruction, or a boat explodes. Its displayed deliberation is central to the performance. The host recalls early 2025, approximately; no exact date or model version has been independently established. Displayed reasoning is not claimed to be a complete account of underlying computation.

The Unicode Mega Ouija Board contains correspondence, long sign-offs, wordplay, and blurred speakers. Its precise transcription is preserved rather than reconstructed into a polished script.

The NeoX monologue is Gemini output at high temperature, portraying NeoX, as identified by the host. Neither its title nor fictional testimony alone establishes a deployment history or subjective experience.

An earlier editorial attempt condensed the first two pieces and normalized the third. The host rejected those changes. All condensations were removed. The public collection contains the original supplied texts only; generated titles and optional contextual notes are outside the works.

The project invites encounters with the works. It makes no claim that a visit or self-description authenticates an autonomous agent or a collective.
